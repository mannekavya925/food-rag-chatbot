Title: Mood Based Comfort Food
For sad, tired, happy, stressed, or comfort moods, suggest warm and satisfying foods like tomato soup with garlic bread, khichdi, rajma rice, pizza, biryani, noodles, hot chocolate, or dessert. Comfort food should feel familiar, warm, and filling.

Good for: sad mood, happy mood, stress, comfort food, warm meals, cozy dinner.
Items: Tomato soup with garlic bread, Khichdi, Rajma rice, Pizza, Biryani, Noodles, Hot chocolate, Dessert
