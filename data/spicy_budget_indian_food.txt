Title: Spicy Budget Indian Food
For spicy food under Rs. 200, suggest chicken biryani, spicy paneer wrap, vada pav, samosa chaat, masala dosa, chilli potato, or egg roll. These are filling, flavorful, and usually easy to find in Indian restaurants or street food shops.

Good for: spicy cravings, low budget meals, lunch, dinner, evening snacks, Indian cuisine.
Items: Chicken biryani, Spicy paneer wrap, Vada pav, Samosa chaat, Masala dosa, Chilli potato, Egg roll
